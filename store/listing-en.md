## Full description (max 4000)
```
Several people putting money in every month — for a plot of land, a shared savings pot, or a rotating fund? Keep one clear record everyone can see, instead of a notebook, a chat thread and somebody's memory.

Who it is for
• A few friends buying land or a flat together, paying monthly installments
• Friends or family saving a fixed amount each month
• A সমিতি (rotating savings fund), where one member receives the pot each month

What it does
• Three kinds of group: installment, savings, rotating fund
• Monthly entries — who paid how much, with a receipt photo and date
• Two-person approval: whoever records an entry is not the one who approves it
• What went to the builder or the bank, and what is still due, on one dashboard
• Roles with real permissions: Creator, Admin, Collector, Member
• An activity log of every change, which nobody can delete
• Monthly reports, downloadable as PDF or Excel
• Bring years of old records in from an Excel sheet in one go
• Bangla and English, switchable any time
• Entries work offline and sync when the connection returns

Transparency is the point
Everyone in the group can see what each member has paid and what the group holds. An entry recorded by one person has to be approved by another, so "but I did pay" stops being an argument. Every change stays in the log.

What this app does not do
• It moves no money — it is a ledger, not a payment app
• No ads
• Your data is never sold or shared

How to use it (SOP): https://kistify.web.app/sop

```
